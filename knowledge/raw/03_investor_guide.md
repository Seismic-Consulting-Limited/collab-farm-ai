# CollabFarm Investor Guide

## Who is an Investor?

An investor on CollabFarm provides funding for agricultural activity by investing in funding packages.

Investors use the platform to discover approved funding packages, review what each package covers, invest, and track investment performance.

## Investor Registration

Investors can create an account on CollabFarm.

After registration:

- The investor account begins in Pending status.
- The investor must be approved before investing.
- Approved investors can access investment features.

## What an Investor Invests In

An investor invests in a funding package, not in an individual farmer.

A funding package is created by a cooperative and covers the farming activity of the farmers enrolled in it. An investor can invest in a single package or in multiple packages.

## Viewing Funding Packages

Investors can browse funding packages that have been approved and are open for funding.

Packages are organised by:

- Farming category, such as crop farming, fishery, poultry, livestock or mixed farming
- Package type: Starter, Growth or Commercial

## The Three Package Types

The package type tells an investor the scale of farming being funded.

Starter packages support small scale farming and cover the essential inputs for a farming cycle, such as seeds, basic fertilizer and land preparation.

Growth packages support larger scale farming and add labour support and basic equipment.

Commercial packages support the largest scale farming and add irrigation, machinery, and harvesting and transport.

The funding amount increases from Starter through Growth to Commercial. Each type has a minimum amount:

- Starter: from 1 million naira
- Growth: from 2 million naira
- Commercial: from 5 million naira

Each package states its own amount, tenure and expected return, and what it covers depends on its farming category.

## Reviewing a Package

Before investing, investors can review:

- The package name and type
- The farming category
- The funding amount
- The tenure of the package
- What the package covers, such as seeds, fertilizer, land preparation, labour or equipment
- The expected return
- The farming cycle and its dates
- The cooperative managing the package
- The number of farmers enrolled
- Package status

Sensitive farmer information, such as NIN, is not exposed to investors.

## Investing

Investors can select an approved package and invest an amount.

The system ensures:

- The investor is approved.
- The investment amount does not exceed the remaining amount required.
- Successful investments are recorded.
- Funding progress updates after investment.

An investor can hold investments in several packages at the same time.

## Tracking Investments

Investors can monitor:

- Active investments
- Completed investments
- Expected returns
- Settled amounts
- Overdue investments
- Investment history

Investors can view investment timelines showing key events such as:

- Investment funding
- Agreement acceptance
- Fund release
- Farming progress
- Harvest
- Settlement

## Investment Agreement

Before funds are released, the investment agreement must be accepted by the required parties.

The agreement contains relevant investment information and terms.

Funds cannot be released before required acceptance is completed.

## Settlement Tracking

Investors can monitor settlement information, including:

- Expected settlement amount
- Actual settlement amount when recorded
- Settlement date
- Settlement status

Completed investments remain available in investment history.
