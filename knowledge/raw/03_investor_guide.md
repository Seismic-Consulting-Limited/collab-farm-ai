# CollabFarm Investor Guide

## Who is an Investor?

An investor on CollabFarm provides funding for individual farmers and agricultural activities.

Investors use the platform to discover approved farming opportunities, review farmer and farm information, invest in farming activities, and track investment performance.

## Investor Registration

Investors can create an account on CollabFarm.

After registration:

- The investor account begins in Pending status.
- The investor must be approved before investing.
- Approved investors can access investment features.

## Viewing Investment Opportunities

Investors can browse approved and open farming opportunities.

Each opportunity may show information such as:

- Farmer reference
- Crop type
- Farm size
- Location information
- Cooperative information
- Production cost
- Farmer contribution where applicable
- Funding requirement
- Amount already funded
- Remaining funding amount

Only approved and open opportunities are available for investment.

## Reviewing Farming Opportunities

Before investing, investors can review:

- Farmer information
- Farm details
- Farming cycle information
- Funding requirement
- Expected agricultural outcome
- Expected settlement information

Sensitive farmer information, such as NIN, is not exposed to investors.

## Investing in Farmers

Investors can select a farming opportunity and invest an amount.

The system ensures:

- The investor is approved.
- The investment amount does not exceed the remaining funding requirement.
- Successful investments are recorded.
- Funding progress updates after investment.

## Tracking Investments

Investors can monitor:

- Active investments
- Completed investments
- Expected returns
- Settled amounts
- Overdue investments
- Investment history

Investors can view investment timelines showing key events such as:

- Investment funding
- Agreement acceptance
- Fund release
- Farming progress
- Harvest
- Settlement

## Investment Agreement

Before funds are released, the investment agreement must be accepted by the required parties.

The agreement contains relevant investment information and terms.

Funds cannot be released before required acceptance is completed.

## Settlement Tracking

Investors can monitor settlement information, including:

- Expected settlement amount
- Actual settlement amount when recorded
- Settlement date
- Settlement status

Completed investments remain available in investment history.
