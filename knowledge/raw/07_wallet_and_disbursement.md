# CollabFarm Funding, Disbursement and Settlement Guide

## Overview

CollabFarm tracks agricultural funding from investor commitment through fund release, farmer disbursement, farming progress and settlement.

The platform ensures that investment activities are connected to verified farmers and documented farming activities.

## Investment Funding

Investors fund approved farmer opportunities through the platform.

The system records:

- Investor information
- Funded opportunity
- Investment amount
- Investment date
- Investment status

Investment amounts cannot exceed the remaining funding requirement of an opportunity.

## Fully Funded Opportunities

When the required funding amount is reached:

- The opportunity status changes to Fully Funded.
- Additional investment is prevented.
- Relevant users are notified.

## Investment Agreement

Before funds are released, required parties must accept the investment agreement.

The agreement contains relevant investment information and terms.

Funds cannot be released before required acceptance.

## Fund Release

After agreement acceptance:

- Approved funds are released according to the investment process.
- The investment record is updated.

## Farmer Disbursement

Cooperative Admins record funds provided to farmers.

Disbursement records include:

- Farmer information
- Investment reference
- Amount
- Date
- Supporting evidence where applicable

The total disbursement cannot exceed available approved funds.

## Disbursement History

Cooperative Admins can view previous disbursement records.

Records include:

- Date
- Farmer
- Amount
- Status

Historical records are retained for transparency.

## Farming Progress Tracking

After funds are disbursed, Cooperative Admins update farming progress.

Progress stages include:

- Not Started
- Planting
- Growing
- Harvesting
- Harvest Completed
- Awaiting Settlement
- Completed

Investors can monitor permitted farming progress information.

## Settlement

Settlement records complete the investment lifecycle.

Settlement information includes:

- Expected settlement amount
- Actual settlement amount
- Settlement date
- Settlement status

Completed investments remain available in investment history.
