# CollabFarm Cooperative Guide

## Who is a Cooperative Admin?

A Cooperative Admin manages farmers and farming activities belonging to the cooperative.

The Cooperative Admin is responsible for maintaining accurate farmer information and preparing farmer funding opportunities for investors.

## Cooperative Profile

A Cooperative Admin can manage cooperative information.

The cooperative profile helps ensure that investors and administrators have accurate cooperative details.

## Adding Farmers

Cooperative Admins can add farmers to CollabFarm.

When adding farmers, the cooperative provides farmer information and the system creates a unique farmer reference.

New farmers begin with a Pending verification status.

## Farmer Verification

Farmer verification ensures that only verified farmers can be considered for funding.

Farmer verification statuses include:

- Pending
- Verified
- Rejected

Only verified farmers can be attached to funding opportunities.

## Farm Registration

After adding a farmer, the Cooperative Admin can record farm information.

Farm information includes:

- Farm size
- Crop information
- Location information

Farm information is linked to the farmer record.

## Farming Cycles

Each farmer funding opportunity is connected to a farming cycle.

A farming cycle defines the agricultural activity period, including:

- Crop
- Start date
- Planting date
- Harvest date
- Settlement date

Closed farming cycles cannot be used for new funding opportunities.

## Creating Farmer Funding Opportunities

Cooperative Admins create funding opportunities based on real farmer farming activities.

A funding opportunity includes:

- Verified farmer
- Farm information
- Farming activity
- Farming cycle
- Farming costs
- Expected agricultural outcomes

The funding requirement is calculated from actual farming information.

## Funding Calculation

CollabFarm calculates funding requirements based on farming economics.

The calculation considers:

- Farm size
- Cost per hectare
- Production costs
- Farmer contribution

The calculated funding requirement must reflect the actual farming activity.

## Cost Breakdown

Cooperative Admins provide a breakdown of farming costs.

Cost categories include:

- Farming expenses
- Amounts required for the activity

The total cost breakdown must match the production cost before submission.

## Submitting Funding Opportunities

Once complete, the Cooperative Admin can submit a funding opportunity for review.

The opportunity moves through approval before becoming available to investors.

## Tracking Funding Activities

Cooperatives can monitor:

- Funding progress
- Funded amount
- Remaining amount
- Investment status

## Farmer Disbursement

After investment funding is received, Cooperative Admins can record disbursements to farmers.

Disbursement records include:

- Farmer
- Amount
- Date
- Investment reference

## Farming Progress

Cooperative Admins can update farming progress.

Progress stages include:

- Not Started
- Planting
- Growing
- Harvesting
- Harvest Completed
- Awaiting Settlement
- Completed

Supporting notes and evidence can also be provided.

## Settlement Updates

Cooperative Admins can record settlement information.

This includes:

- Settlement amount
- Settlement date
- Settlement status

Completed investments are marked as Completed.
